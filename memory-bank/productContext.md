# Product Context

## Why this project exists
This project aims to establish an online presence for a cleaning service, enabling it to reach a wider audience and streamline its customer acquisition and booking processes.

## Problems it solves
*   For customers: Difficulty in finding reliable cleaning services, lack of transparency in pricing, and inconvenient booking procedures.
*   For the business: Limited reach, manual booking inefficiencies, and a need for a professional platform to showcase services and build trust.

## How it should work
The website will serve as a digital storefront. Potential clients will visit the landing page to understand the services offered, use a cost calculator for estimates, fill out an online application for booking, and book available slots. Users can also access a personal account to view their order history and leave reviews. The website will be built on WordPress with integrated online payment and multi-language support (Russian, English, Serbian).

## User Experience Goals
*   **Premium and Solid:** Convey a sense of professionalism, reliability, and high quality.
*   **Efficient:** Allow users to quickly find information, calculate costs, and book services with minimal friction.
*   **User-Friendly:** Intuitive navigation and clear calls to action across all features.
*   **Trustworthy:** Build confidence through clear service descriptions, testimonials (reviews), and guaranteed results.
