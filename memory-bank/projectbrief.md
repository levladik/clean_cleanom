# Project Brief

## Project Purpose
To promote a cleaning service.

## Website Type
Single-page landing page.

## Main Goal
Presentation of services.

## Target Audience
*   **Individuals:** 18–65 years old, employed people (families with children, IT specialists, businesspeople).
*   **Legal Entities:** Offices, cafes/restaurants, salons, landlords, construction companies.
*   **Location:** Belgrade + suburbs (up to 30 km).

## Client Priorities
*   Quality of cleaning
*   Guaranteed results
*   Punctuality
*   Neatness

## Essential Features
*   Online application (one-time/regular) with a questionnaire.
*   Cost calculator.
*   Personal account (order history, reviews).
*   Slot booking with notes and cancellation (with time limit).

## Brand Style Guide
*   **Colors:** Green, black, white.
*   **Accents:** Leaves.
*   **Style:** Premium, solid.
*   **Reference Websites:** qlean.ru, pchelki-cleaning.ru.

## Integrations
*   Online payment.
*   WordPress.

## Supported Languages
*   Russian
*   English
*   Serbian
